### I. Holistic Architecture over Reductionism
Reductionist approaches attempt to solve complex governance challenges by dissecting them into microscopic, unrelated parts. Systems governance does the exact opposite. A change in taxpayer digital onboarding directly alters audit risk profiles downstream; therefore, policy design must evaluate the entire macro-system simultaneously.

### II. Dynamic Feedback Loops
Static rules create rigid bureaucracies. Modern revenue administration requires continuous feedback loops where real-world compliance data, audit findings, and operational friction flow back into policy formulation.
