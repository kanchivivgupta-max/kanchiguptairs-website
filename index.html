About Me

I am a professional operating at the intersection of technology, law, and public administration. As a member of the Indian Revenue Service (IRS) with a B.Tech in Electrical Engineering and an LL.B. (Bachelor of Laws), I specialize in analyzing complex systems, understanding regulatory frameworks, and designing structured problem-solving models.

My professional journey spans public administration, indirect taxation, customs, and trade facilitation, including key field assignments such as serving as a Specified Officer at the SEEPZ Special Economic Zone in Mumbai. This unique blend of engineering fundamentals, legal analysis, and revenue administration drives my interest in modern software design, technical documentation, and structured learning.

This website serves as my personal digital hub, a professional portfolio, and an open-access repository where I share my detailed, structured study notes on software engineering, technical frameworks, and stateful multi-agent workflows.
