---
title: "The Brave Little Butterfly"
description: "Follow Bella the butterfly on a sensory journey of fluttering wings, warm sunshine, and gentle flower landing actions!"
icon: "🦋"
---
Quote: "Look! A tiny, sleeping green caterpillar in a cozy brown shell."
Action: Touch your finger to your hand and pretend to sleep: "Shh, sleeping!"
Sound: Soft snoring: "Zzz, zzz, zzz."

---
Quote: "Crack! Bella the butterfly opens her wings! One, two, three!"
Action: Flap your arms slowly like butterfly wings together.
Sound: "Flutter, flutter, flutter!"

---
Quote: "Oh! The wind is blowing Bella high into the sky!"
Action: Stretch your arms way up high to touch the clouds.
Sound: Take a deep breath: "Whoosh, whoosh!"

---
Quote: "Time to rest! Bella lands softly on a sweet, pretty pink flower."
Action: Tap your finger gently onto your toddler's knee: "Soft landing!"
Sound: Sniff deeply: "Mmm, smells so pretty!"
