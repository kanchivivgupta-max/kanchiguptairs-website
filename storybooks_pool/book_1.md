---
title: "The Three Little Pigs"
description: "An interactive, printable sensory guide for parents and toddlers to count, tap, and knock together while building houses!"
icon: "🐷"
---
Quote: "Look! Three happy little pigs! One, two, three!"
Action: Point to each pig and count together with your toddler.
Sound: "Oink, oink, oink!"

---
Quote: "This pig is building a house. It is made of yellow straw!"
Action: Tap the yellow straw on the page together.
Sound: "Swish, swish, swish!"

---
Quote: "This pig is building a house. It is made of wooden sticks!"
Action: Point to the tree branches and wooden sticks.
Sound: Tap on the table: "Tap, tap, tap!"

---
Quote: "This pig is building a strong house. It is made of red bricks!"
Action: Knock on the floor or table: "Is it strong?"
Sound: "Clink, clink, clink!"

---
Quote: "Blow, blow, blow! Can you blow the strong brick house down?"
Action: Take a deep breath and pretend to blow together.
Sound: "Whoosh, whoosh!" (No, it is too strong!)
