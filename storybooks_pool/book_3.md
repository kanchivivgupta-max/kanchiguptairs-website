---
title: "The Little Red Hen"
description: "A printable, interactive sensory tale for toddlers to plant seeds, grind flour, and enjoy warm baking smells!"
icon: "🐔"
---
Quote: "Look! A busy little red hen. She is planting tiny yellow seeds."
Action: Tap your fingers on the floor like planting seeds.
Sound: "Chirp, chirp, chirp!"

---
Quote: "Cut, cut, cut! The wheat is tall. Let's harvest the golden stalks!"
Action: Pretend to cut wheat stalks with your hand.
Sound: "Swish, swish, swish!"

---
Quote: "Grind, grind! Turn the wheel to make the wheat into white flour."
Action: Roll your hands around and around in a big circle.
Sound: Rub your hands together: "Grind, grind, grind!"

---
Quote: "Bake, bake! Pour the flour into a big bowl and mix it up!"
Action: Pretend to stir a giant bowl of dough with a big spoon.
Sound: "Stir, stir, splat!"

---
Quote: "Mmm, look! The bread is warm, fresh, and smelling so sweet!"
Action: Blow gently on your toddler's palm to cool the warm bread.
Sound: Take a big breath: "Ahhh, delicious!"
