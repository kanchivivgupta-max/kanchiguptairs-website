Goodnight, Barnaby Bear!

An Interactive Bedtime Storybook featuring Barnaby the Bear

Sleep tight, Barnaby Bear!

"Playtime is over! The warm sun goes down. Bye-bye, sun!"

Action Wave goodbye to the window or sky
Sound "Yawn... Chime, chime!"
Page 1

"Splish, splash! Barnaby takes a warm bubble bath!"

Action Gently tap the water on the page or splash fingers in the air
Sound "Splash, splash! Squeak!"
Page 2

"Put on the cozy yellow pajamas! Soft and warm!"

Action Run your hand down the child's arm to mimic putting on sleeves
Sound "Zip, zip! Snuggly!"
Page 3

"Brush the teeth clean! Brush up and down. Sparkle!"

Action Make a brushing motion on your teeth
Sound "Swish, swish, swish!"
Page 4

"Choose a bedtime storybook. Point to the blue book!"

Action Point to the blue book on the shelf
Sound "Turn the page: rustle, rustle!"
Page 5

"Climb into the cozy wooden bed. Crawl, crawl!"

Action Walk two fingers slowly across the page to the bed
Sound "Creep, creep! Hop!"
Page 6

"Tuck under the soft blue blanket. Pat, pat! Warm and snug!"

Action Pretend to pull a blanket up to the child's chin and pat it gently
Sound "Sigh... Snuggle!"
Page 7

"Goodnight, Barnaby Bear! Shh... close your eyes. Sleep tight!"

Action Close your eyes and whisper "goodnight"
Sound "Shh... Goodnight. Sleepy breathing."
