Brush, Brush, Brush Your Teeth!

An Interactive Toddler Storybook featuring Dino the Dinosaur

Brushing Teeth Keeps Dino's Smile Happy!

"Dino the dinosaur eats a sweet red apple! Crunch, crunch, crunch!"

Action Pretend to hold an apple and take a big bite
Sound "Crunch, crunch, crunch! Yum!"
Page 1

"Uh-oh, apple in Dino's teeth! Time to brush!"

Action Point to your own teeth and shake your head
Sound "Uh-oh! Dirty teeth!"
Page 2

"Pick up the little yellow toothbrush! Hold it tight!"

Action Pretend to pick up a toothbrush and hold it in your hand
Sound "Got it! Yay!"
Page 3

"Squeeze a tiny drop of white toothpaste. Squeeze! Plop!"

Action Pretend to squeeze a tube of toothpaste onto a brush
Sound "Squeeeeeze! Plop!"
Page 4

"Brush the front teeth! Up and down, up and down!"

Action Move your finger up and down in front of your mouth
Sound "Brush, brush, brush!"
Page 5

"Brush the back teeth! Round and round, round and round!"

Action Move your finger in circles near your cheek
Sound "Tickle, tickle! Scrub, scrub!"
Page 6

"Take a sip of water. Swish, swish... now spit! Ptu!"

Action Pretend to take a drink, puff your cheeks, and then pretend to spit
Sound "Swish, swish... Ptu!"
Page 7

"Look at the clean, shiny teeth! What a happy smile! Clap, clap!"

Action Give your child a big high-five and clap hands
Sound "Sparkle, sparkle! Hooray!"
