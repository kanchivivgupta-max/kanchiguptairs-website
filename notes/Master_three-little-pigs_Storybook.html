The Three Little Pigs

An Interactive Toddler Storybook

An Interactive Guide for Parents & Toddlers

"Look! Three happy little pigs! One, two, three!"

Action Point to each pig and count together
Sound "Oink, oink, oink!"
Page 1

"This pig is building a house. It is made of yellow straw!"

Action Tap the yellow straw on the page
Sound "Swish, swish, swish!"
Page 2

"This pig is building a house. It is made of wooden sticks!"

Action Point to the tree branches and sticks
Sound Tap on table: "Tap, tap, tap!"
Page 3

"This pig is building a strong house. It is made of red bricks!"

Action Knock on the floor or table: "Is it strong?"
Sound "Clink, clink, clink!"
Page 4

"Oh look! A big grey wolf is walking here!"

Action Touch the wolf's big black nose
Sound Playful growling: "Grrr..."
Page 5

"The wolf goes... HUFF and PUFF! The straw house flies away!"

Action Blow a gentle puff of air at their cheek
Sound Deep breath: "Fuuuuuuh! Whoosh!"
Page 6

"The wolf blows again! HUFF and PUFF! The stick house falls down!"

Action Wave hands together like flying sticks
Sound "Fuuuuuuh! Crash!"
Page 7

"The wolf blows and blows. But the brick house is too strong!"

Action Gently wiggle toddler: "No, no, no, it won't fall!"
Sound Exhausted wolf: "Phew! I'm tired!"
Page 8

"The wolf climbs up. Ouch! The soup is hot! Go away, wolf!"

Action Pretend to jump up surprised
Sound "Sssss! Ouch!"
Page 9

"Yay! The pigs are safe. They are dancing! Hooray!"

Action Clap hands and do a little wiggle dance
Sound Cheer: "Hooray! Yay!"
