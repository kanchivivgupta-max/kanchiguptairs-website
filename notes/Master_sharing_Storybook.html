Sharing is Fun!

An Interactive Toddler Storybook with Pip and Pop

Sharing Our Toys Keeps Pip and Pop Happy!

"Pip the puppy and Pop the panda meet at the park! Wave, wave!"

Action Wave a hand to greet Pip and Pop
Sound "Hi, Pip! Hi, Pop! Woof!"
Page 1

"Pop the panda has a bright red ball! Bounce, bounce!"

Action Tap the red ball on the page with a finger
Sound "Boing, boing!"
Page 2

"Roll the red ball to Pip. Roll, roll! Here you go!"

Action Slide your finger across the page from Pop to Pip
Sound "Zoooom! Thank you!"
Page 3

"Pip has colorful toy blocks! One, two, three blocks!"

Action Point to and count the yellow, blue, and red blocks
Sound "Clink, clack! One, two, three!"
Page 4

"Build a tall tower together. Up, up! So high!"

Action Trace a finger straight up in the air to show the height of the tower
Sound "Up, up, up!"
Page 5

"The big tower falls down! Crash! Oh no!"

Action Clap hands once loudly and look surprised
Sound "Crash! Boom! Giggles!"
Page 6

"Pop has a sweet banana cookie. Break it in half. Snap!"

Action Pretend to break a cookie in half with both hands
Sound "Snap! Mmm, yummy!"
Page 7

"Pip and Pop are best friends! Sharing is fun! Hug!"

Action Give your child a big hug or hold hands
Sound "Yay! Clap, clap! Woof!"
