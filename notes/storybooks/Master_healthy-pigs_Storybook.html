Three Little Pigs Grow Big and Strong

An Interactive Toddler Storybook about Good Food

Eating Healthy Makes Us Strong!

"Meet the three little pigs! They want to grow big and strong!"

Action Point to the apple and carrot on the table
Sound Rub tummy: "Yum, yum, yum!"
Page 1

"Look! This pig eats green broccoli. Crunch, crunch! Now he is strong!"

Action Touch child's arm muscles: "So strong!"
Sound "Crunch, crunch, crunch!"
Page 2

"This pig eats a sweet red apple. Munch, munch! He is full of energy!"

Action Reach hands up high like the apple tree
Sound "Munch, munch, munch!"
Page 3

"This pig eats warm carrot soup. Slurp, slurp! He is super strong!"

Action Pretend to hold a bowl and blow to cool it down
Sound "Sluuuuurp!"
Page 4

"Oh look! The big grey wolf walks up. He is hungry!"

Action Make a surprised face and point to the wolf's tail
Sound Funny howl: "Awooooo!"
Page 5

"The wolf blows... HUFF and PUFF! But the brick house is too strong!"

Action Blow a gentle puff of air at toddler's hand
Sound Deep breath and blow: "Fuuuuuuh!"
Page 6

"The pigs push the door shut. Push, push! They are so strong!"

Action Push hands gently against toddler's hands
Sound Grunt of effort: "Ugh, ugh! Strong!"
Page 7

"The wolf is tired. He sits down. Bye-bye, wolf!"

Action Wave goodbye: "Bye-bye, wolf!"
Sound Yawn loudly: "Ahhh, so tired!"
Page 8

"Yay! The pigs eat a big yummy dinner. We love good food!"

Action Clap hands and cheer together
Sound "Hooray! Yay!"
